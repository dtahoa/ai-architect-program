# Pass/Fail Criteria

## Pass

A submission passes when it provides a coherent architecture, addresses major risks, includes required deliverables, and clearly explains the rationale behind important decisions.

## Fail

A submission fails when critical requirements are missing, architecture decisions are unsupported, AI risks are ignored, or the design cannot reasonably meet the stated product goals.

## Review Notes

Use the scoring rubric to support the pass/fail decision.
