# Scoring Rubric

## Categories

| Category | Description | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem Framing | Clarity of user, business, and technical context | 20 |
| Architecture Quality | Coherence, scalability, reliability, and tradeoff handling | 30 |
| AI Design | Model, data, prompting, evaluation, and workflow choices | 25 |
| Governance and Operations | Security, privacy, observability, and maintainability | 15 |
| Communication | Clarity of diagrams, ADRs, and presentation | 10 |

## Total

100 points
