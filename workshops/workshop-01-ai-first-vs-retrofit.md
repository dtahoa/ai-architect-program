# Workshop 01: AI-First vs Retrofit

## Objective

Compare AI-first architecture with AI-retrofitted architecture for a real product scenario.

## Activities

- Analyze an existing product workflow.
- Identify opportunities and constraints for AI integration.
- Propose an AI-first architecture.
- Compare tradeoffs against a retrofit approach.

## Deliverables

- Architecture comparison notes
- Key tradeoff list
- Recommended approach
